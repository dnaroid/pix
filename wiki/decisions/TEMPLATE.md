# NNNN — Decision title

This is a template, not an accepted decision. Copy it to a new file and replace
all placeholders; omit only genuinely inapplicable details with a short reason.

- Status: proposed | accepted | superseded | withdrawn
- Recorded: YYYY-MM-DD
- Decided: YYYY-MM-DD, or unknown (identify retrospective records)
- Owner / approval evidence: …
- Governing spec: relative Markdown link to the current spec
- Replaces / replaced by: none, or relative links

## Context

Problem, constraints and scope at the time of the choice.

## Observations and sources

Verified facts and links to evidence. Separately label reported observations,
assumptions and missing evidence. Conversation-only sources must be attributed;
never invent a durable artifact or measurements.

## Decision

Chosen approach, scope and explicit exceptions.

## Alternatives

Plausible options and why they were not chosen; unknown reasoning stays unknown.

## Consequences

Expected benefits, costs, risks and uncertainties, not assumed proven outcomes.

## Revisit when

Concrete signals or evidence that would justify reconsideration.
